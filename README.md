# Personal Expense Tracker System

A system that helps individuals manage their personal finances by recording income and expenses, organising transactions, and monitoring their remaining balance.

## Problem Statement

Individuals who manage their personal finances using notebooks or spreadsheets may experience difficulties tracking their daily income and expenses. Manually recording financial transactions can result in incomplete records, calculation errors and difficulties organising financial information. As a result, users may struggle to monitor their spending habits, calculate their remaining balance and understand their overall financial position. This makes personal financial management less efficient and may affect their ability to make informed financial decisions.
