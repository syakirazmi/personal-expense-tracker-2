# Problem Statement

## Who is affected?

Individuals who manage their personal finances and want to monitor their daily income and expenses.

## What exactly is going wrong?

Users who rely on manual methods such as notebooks or spreadsheets may find it difficult to consistently record transactions, organise expenses and calculate their remaining balance. Manual tracking can also lead to incomplete records and calculation errors.

## Why does it matter?

Without organised financial records, users may struggle to understand their spending habits, monitor their financial position and make informed financial decisions.

## Problem statement (3 to 5 sentences)

Without organised financial records, users may struggle to understand their spending habits, monitor their financial position and make informed financial decisions.
